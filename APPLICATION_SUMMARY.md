## Interview opener (45–60 seconds)
ATOZ E-File is a tax operations platform I'm building to simplify client intake, document management and preparer workflows. The technical challenge is not just creating forms—it's enforcing secure data boundaries while supporting an easy client experience. I designed the application around Next.js and PostgreSQL with database-level row security, role-aware workflows, private storage and automated tests. A recent example was redesigning an authorization gate so clients could save their own draft intake without opening staff access before agreements were signed. That change passed local tests and is awaiting controlled deployment. The next phase adds source-grounded AI and tax-software integrations with professional review, and the longer-term roadmap includes iOS and Android apps sharing the same backend.
